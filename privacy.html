Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026 • Effective date: November 23, 2026

Summary: Stitchify is 100% client-side. We do not operate any tracking servers, analytics, or remote image processing. Every screenshot, annotation, and export stays strictly on your computer in local memory and browser storage (IndexedDB).

1. 100% Client-Side & Local Processing

Stitchify is built from the ground up to respect user confidentiality.

  • Zero Remote Servers: No backend APIs, telemetry endpoints, or external processing pipelines exist.
  • Zero Tracking or Analytics: We do not log usage events, IP addresses, browsing habits, or device telemetry.
  • Zero Cloud Image Storage: Screenshots taken through Stitchify are never transmitted across the network.

2. Data We Process and Where It Lives

When using Stitchify, all operations occur solely within your browser:

📸 Captured Viewports

Captured images are encoded as data URLs and saved in your local IndexedDB (stitchify-db) so you can arrange and stitch them.

🏷️ Page Titles & URLs

Saved only alongside each thumbnail in local storage to label your captures and name exported files (e.g., stitched-screenshot-*.png).

✏️ Annotation Layers

Arrows, shapes, callout text, and pen strokes are rendered on local HTML5 Canvas and saved in IndexedDB for session continuity.

⚙️ User Preferences

Gap spacing, canvas padding, and background color choices are stored locally using client-side IndexedDB and theme settings in localStorage.

3. Browser Permissions Explained

Permission Purpose & Justification
activeTab Allows visible tab capture and page metadata retrieval when you initiate a screenshot.
scripting Reads window.devicePixelRatio to render stitched images at native HiDPI resolution.
sidePanel Displays the capture reel in a persistent dock adjacent to your web tabs.
<all_urls> Enables visible viewport capture across any website via the side panel and keyboard shortcuts. In Chromium MV3, side panels do not grant activeTab permissions on user clicks.

4. User Control & Data Deletion

You retain absolute control over all data created with Stitchify. You can delete single captures by clicking the trash icon in the side panel or editor, or clear all captures with one click. Uninstalling Stitchify or clearing your browser's site storage instantly and permanently purges all local data.
